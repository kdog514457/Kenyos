Our Story

About KENYOS

Who We Are

A curated vision for interior excellence

KENYOS is a premium interior and building solutions company. We were founded on a single belief: every space — residential, commercial, or institutional — deserves exceptional materials, thoughtful design, and access to the world's finest brands.

We provide a complete, curated source for interior finishing — kitchens, wardrobes, Empolo bathrooms, door systems, wood solutions, and CHINT smart electrical infrastructure. One destination. Every interior need.

Our showroom is not a warehouse. It is a living environment where clients walk through real kitchen environments, bathroom suites, and brand zones, guided by specialists who understand their project from concept to completion.

"Every space has the potential to be extraordinary. Our role is to provide the materials, the expertise, and the vision to make that possible — for every client, on every project."
— KENYOS Design & Interiors Philosophy
